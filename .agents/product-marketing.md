# Product Marketing Context

## Business Overview
**Brand:** RoutineForge / CortexFlow
**Service:** AI & Automation Agency
**Core Value Proposition:** Helping businesses cut costs and optimize workflows by automating repetitive tasks (e.g., data entry, system integration, answering routine client inquiries, handling bookings).

## Target Audience (ICP)
- Dentists and Healthcare Clinics
- Tour Agencies
- Restaurants
- Hotels
- Real Estate Agencies
- E-commerce Brands
- Instagram Shops
- Law Firms

## Customer Research & Psychology
**Common Pain Points:**
- Overwhelmed by repetitive tasks and manual data entry.
- Disconnected systems that don't talk to each other.
- Wasting staff time replying to the exact same or similar client inquiries.
- Managing bookings manually.

**Common Objections & Friction Points:**
1. **Cost:** "It's too expensive to implement AI/automation."
2. **Skepticism:** "Does this actually work for my specific business?"
3. **Support/Risk:** "What happens if the automation breaks? Will I be left without support?"

## Content Strategy Focus
- **Primary Format:** Industry-benchmark case studies.
- **Goal:** Drive traffic (searchable) and demonstrate authority/ROI (shareable).
- **Core Metrics Highlighted:** Time saved (staff focusing on productive topics), money gained, error reduction.
- **Workflow:** Publish comprehensive case studies on the blog, then atomize into summary posts for LinkedIn to drive traffic back to the site.
